Synoptic Diff

Prototype · Layer 2

The gospels narrate the same events in different orders, with different wording, and sometimes fuse two scenes into one. Printed synopses have set them side by side since Eusebius numbered the sections in the 300s. This does it as a diff — structure first, then wording — and then reads the passage at four depths.
